Welcome to Your 52-Week Journey

Thank you for choosing this guide. Opening the Scriptures can feel like walking into a great church for the first time: beautiful, overwhelming, and a little hard to find your place. You don't have to find it alone.

This companion was made to walk beside you through all fifty-two weeks. Listen to each week on your drive or during a quiet evening, print the pages you need, and bring the questions that come up to your priest and your parish. There is no rush and no falling behind. Wherever you are right now is a good place to begin.

May every week bring you a little closer to understanding what you read, and a little closer to Christ, who speaks through every page.

With prayers for your journey,Simon Alden
